Do you offer free quotes?
Yes. Every quote is free and comes with no obligation. Call, message or use the form below and we'll get back to you.
Are you insured?
Yes. Easy Reach Ltd is fully insured, so you can relax while we work.
Do I need to be home?
Often not. For most outside jobs we can get on while you're out. We'll agree access and anything else we need when we quote.
What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?
Pressure washing uses high-pressure water to blast dirt from tough hard surfaces like driveways, patios and paths. Soft washing uses low pressure with a cleaning treatment to gently lift algae, moss and lichen from roofs, render and other delicate surfaces, and it helps keep them away for longer.
Can you clean my conservatory roof?
Yes. Green, grimy conservatory roofs are one of the most satisfying jobs we do. Have a play with the before and after slider above to see the difference.
Do you work for landlords and businesses?
Yes. We look after landlords and commercial properties as well as homes. Get in touch and we'll talk through what you need.
Which areas do you cover?
