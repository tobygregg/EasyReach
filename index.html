Ask us anything
Do you offer free quotes?
Yes. Every quote is free and comes with no obligation. Call, message or use the form below and we'll get back to you.
Are you insured?
Yes. Easy Reach Ltd is fully insured, so you can relax while we work.
What jobs do you cover besides window cleaning?
Plenty. As well as window cleaning, pressure washing, soft washing and fascia and gutter cleaning, we now handle general property maintenance and repairs, painting and decorating, fencing and exterior repairs, driveways, patios and paths, conservatory and roof cleaning, landlord and letting-agent maintenance, end-of-tenancy work and commercial property maintenance.
Do I need to be home?
Often not. For most outside jobs we can get on while you're out. We'll agree access and anything else we need when we quote.
What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?
Pressure washing uses high-pressure water to blast dirt from tough hard surfaces like driveways, patios and paths. Soft washing uses low pressure with a cleaning treatment to gently lift algae, moss and lichen from roofs, render and other delicate surfaces, and it helps keep them away for longer.
Can you clean my conservatory roof?
Yes. Yes. We clean the roof panels, frames and bars. The before and after slider above shows a typical result.
Do you work for landlords, letting agents and businesses?
Yes. We look after landlords, letting agents and commercial properties as well as homes, including end-of-tenancy work and regular ongoing maintenance. Get in touch and we'll talk through what you need.
Which areas of Guernsey do you cover?
We're based in Guernsey and cover the whole island, including St Peter Port, St Sampson, the Vale, the Castel, St Saviour, St Pierre du Bois, Torteval, the Forest, St Martin and St Andrew. Not sure we reach you? Just ask.
